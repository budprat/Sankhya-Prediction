## 2. The quake atlas — 13,339 event charts

`scripts/quake_atlas.py` (new). Casts a full chart per event **at its own
epicenter**, then grades every regularity the census surfaces against
era-matched controls. Deliberately two separated parts, because merging them
is how a census becomes a false discovery.

| Corpus | raw → declustered | selected on |
|---|---:|---|
| `usgs-m7` | 1,548 → 1,435 | magnitude M7.0–9.5 |
| `usgs-m6` | 12,212 → 10,324 | magnitude M6.0–6.99 |
| `quakes-ncei-deaths` | 1,981 → 1,553 | **deaths** |
| `quakes-historical` | 39 → 27 | curation |

**Four screens, four nulls**, every power check passing:

| Corpus | n | best lift | null median | family-wise p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M7+ | 1,435 | 1.665 | 1.609 | 0.302 |
| M6 | 10,324 | 1.233 | 1.188 | 0.080 |
| deaths | 1,553 | 1.686 | 1.569 | 0.162 |
| curated | 27 | 11.448 | 11.448 | *uninterpretable* |

### What the controls proved

| Predicate | event rate | control rate | lift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `band:Uranus=22` | 6.5% | **6.6%** | 0.996 |
| `sep:Sun-Mercury@conj` | 7.2% | **9.2%** | 0.783 |

Uranus's most-occupied band looked like a 1.8× enrichment over uniform; it is
its 84-year period sampled over 120 years. Sun ☌ Mercury is the most frequent
contact in the whole census and is *rarer* at quakes than at ordinary
instants, because Mercury never exceeds 28° elongation.

## 3. The band trigger, settled

`PLAN.md` §3.2c. Pre-registered in `scripts/band_trigger_m6.py` and
**committed with no results in it** (`a35d64c`, 11:23:59Z) before the run.

| | M7+ (n = 1,435) | M6.0–6.99 (n = 10,324) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 primary | lift 1.804, p = 0.069 | **lift 0.915, p = 0.76** |
| firings vs expected | 12 vs 7.0 (+1.4 σ) | **38 vs 42.4 (−0.71 σ)** |

Wrong direction. And the atlas explains the original near-miss: the trigger
fires at 0.84% of M7+ events, 0.37% of M6 events, and **0.41% of M6 controls**.
The base rate is ~0.41%, so the M7+ figure was a 2× upward fluctuation on
twelve events — exactly what 1.4 σ meant.

**Cost recorded:** M6.0–6.99 was the project's only strictly disjoint held-out
band. It has now been used twice and is no longer clean for confirmatory work.

## 4. Pattern mining, and three artifacts caught

`scripts/pattern_mine_m7.py` (new). M7+ only. Two genuinely new things:

**A unified M7+ corpus.** All three catalogs that carry M7+ events,
deduplicated 3 d / 300 km: 1,548 + 499 + 35 → 447 duplicates merged → **1,635
unique**, 1,506 post-1900 declustered. Larger than any previous M7+ run,
because the deaths file holds M7+ events the USGS magnitude file misses.

**Co-occurrence search.** Every prior screen tested *single* predicates, but no
taught pattern is single — Nepal is *real-Nep-on-Ketu AND real-Ura-on-Sun*. So
pairs and triples were searched: 609 + 181 + 15 = 805 patterns.

### The first run said SUPPORTED (p = 0.0067). It was wrong three times.

1. **Era-locked predicates.** Winner `band:Neptune=24 + mkm≤60` had all 18
   events in **1996–2002** — one Neptune band-dwell. Neptune moves 7.7°/yr
   against a 12.86° band, so a ±365 d control cannot leave it: 96.8% of blocks
   had all four slots identical, contributing zero null variance while still
   driving the observed lift. → **testability filter** on measured
   discrimination, which drops exactly `band:Neptune` (0.46) and `band:Uranus`
   (0.64) and nothing else.
2. **Pseudo-replication.** Next winner `band:Jupiter=8 + band:Saturn=23`, 17
   events in **three distinct years**. For a slow-body predicate the unit of
   independence is the epoch. → **distinct-epoch count**.
3. **Non-replication.** That winner splits **0.998 / 3.394** across 2-year
   blocks. → **mandatory split-half**.

**Final: NOT SUPPORTED.** Family-wise p = 0.010 clears the bar, but the
pattern achieving it fails both new filters; the best pattern that *is*
independent and replicating reaches lift 1.960 against a null-max median of
**1.957** — exactly at chance.

### The structural limit, which may matter more than the verdict

The support floor is 1% (15 events). **The taught patterns are rarer than
that** — `nepal-double` fires at 1 event in 1,506, Chatur Vyuham at 0. This
screen does not refute the doctrine's own rules; **it cannot see them**.
Settling a 0.1% rule at these lifts needs ~10⁵ M7+ events, which do not exist.
No amount of mining this catalogue will ever validate the taught patterns.

Saved as `atlas-patterns.toml` — top 20 in the project's own schema, verified
to load with `triggers.load_rules()`, each carrying lift, support, epoch
count, split-half lifts and a `SURVIVOR` / `FAILS REPLICATION` tag.

## 5. The validation framework, made real

The audit's first finding: `validation.py` was imported by nothing but its own
test, while `TESTING.md` claimed "you cannot run a test here without having
declared what would refute it."

**All 15 grading scripts now construct a `Claim`** and print its banner. The
eight pre-framework scripts were run before and after the port and diffed line
by line — **all eight byte-identical** apart from the banner. Their designs are
transcribed from their own prose headers, not idealised, and each carries its
result in `notes`.

**New: `preregistered` is a required field with no default.** A design written
after its result is a legitimate record but not evidence of the same weight,
and a retrospective declaration that silently reads like a pre-registration is
the exact failure the framework exists to prevent. `report()` now appends
*"this is a LEAD, not a finding"* to any positive result carrying
`preregistered=False`. Two new tests pin both behaviours.

**One implementation of the statistic.** The add-one smoothed lift had been
copy-pasted into six places with identical arithmetic; all now call
`validation.smoothed_lift`, including `signatures.mine_lifts`.

## 6. What this session changed about the project's standing

- **Three excuses spent.** "Wrong category" was already gone; this session
  spent "no held-out data" (§3) and "wrong selection variable" (§2 — deaths-
  and magnitude-selected populations are indistinguishable: doctrine rules
  fire at 3.0/1.4/1.6/0.8/0.6/0.1% vs 2.9/1.5/1.1/0.8/0.6/0.1%).
- **The last data-answerable question is closed**, null. What remains open in
  `PLAN.md` §5 is blocked on NU — the latitude question above all.
- **Two new methodological guards** now protect every future run: the
  testability filter and the epoch/replication requirement. Both were earned
  by a false positive this session actually produced.
- **The negative record grew from 12 channels to 13**, every one power-checked.

### What did not change

The timing core. Every taught instant still reproduces to 1–2 minutes; those
are retrodictions of specific charts and nothing in this session touched them.
The rarity census strengthens them if anything: Chatur Vyuham fires at 0 of
1,435 M7+ events, which is exactly the selectivity the doctrine claims.
