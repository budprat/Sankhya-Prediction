# Plan — where the engine stands and what comes next

Status as of 2026-08-05. Companion to `FRAMEWORK.md` (the theory),
`WATCHLIST.md` (registered forward windows), and the decision ledger at
`.claude/tasks/ASTGRAF_TOOL.md` (every ruling, dated). 239 tests passing.

## 1. The one-line state

**The engine predicts *when* with tested precision, and cannot yet predict
*where* at all.** That is a measured statement, not an impression: every
taught instant reproduces to 1–2 minutes, and six independent location
families have been built and graded null, each with a power check.

**The 130-year sweep result:** anchor patterns are fingerprints. Zero
re-formations away from their own events, at both full and n−1 match, across
ten patterns, 1900–2030. Zero false positives in 130 years — which also
means zero independent trials, so predictive skill is *undemonstrated*, not
demonstrated.

## 3. What is closed

**Location:** six families, six nulls (table in `FRAMEWORK.md`). The
geometric route is exhausted by derivation, not just by testing — see the
clocks argument in §1b.

**Mining:** three passes, three no-survivor verdicts, the last on the
richest feature space the project has had (four-giant real positions,
380 predicates): max lift 1.705 vs null median 1.748, p = 0.65.

**Dwell:** construction reproduces (Nepal 4.14 min vs the author's "4
minutes"), both doctrine halves closed — the trigger half vacuous, the
magnitude half dead on pre-registered held-out M6.0–6.99 data.

## 4. Method rulings now standing

1. **Pre-register cell, direction and statistic before running any lead**,
   and commit the pre-registration before the result exists. This converted
   the dwell lead from an argument into one clean kill.
2. **Every null needs a power check.** A null without demonstrated power is
   silence, not evidence.
3. **Replication is not confirmation.** The dwell finding survived three
   kill attempts and two independent replications and was still false. Only
   held-out data settled it.
4. **Held-out sets must be strictly disjoint.** M6+ contains M7+; the test
   used M6.0–6.99.
5. **Honest negatives stay on the record**, with their numbers.

## 7. The question that unblocks location

The author's recipe has **three longitude mechanisms** (rotation, ecliptic
tilt, light travel) and **one latitude mechanism** (declination), capped at
23.71° for the located set against Nepal's 28.23°N. Until we know what
supplies the latitude, there is nothing left to test — five geometric
families and one empirical family have been exhausted.
